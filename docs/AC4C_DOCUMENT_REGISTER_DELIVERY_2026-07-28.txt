--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Persistence for the payloads the AC-4B formatters produce: an immutable,
per-invoice legal record (e_invoice_documents) with a lifecycle
(generated → submitted → cancelled; regenerating supersedes the prior snapshot),
written only through SECURITY DEFINER RPCs gated by sales.write.

METADATA ONLY — posts NO journal entry, reads/writes NO general_ledger, changes
NO posting RPC and NO existing table. No period-lock gate (you e-invoice after
confirming, often after the period is locked).
