--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An asset register + a monthly depreciation engine that POSTS real journal
entries, plus disposal. Straight-line + reducing-balance (WDV), monthly, pro-rata
by days in the acquisition month, never below salvage.

Posting REUSES the existing post_journal_entry() primitive (which enforces
balance, period lock, JE numbering and audit) — so NO new GL-writing path is
introduced. Dr 6750 Depreciation Expense / Cr 1790 Accumulated Depreciation per
asset per month; disposal removes cost + accum-dep, books proceeds, and
recognises 4250 Gain / 6910 Loss.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE ACCOUNTING (what posts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Monthly depreciation (per asset):  Dr 6750  /  Cr 1790  = period charge.
Disposal:  Dr 1790 (accum to date) + Dr <proceeds acct> (proceeds)
           Cr <asset acct> (cost)  +  Cr 4250 gain  OR  Dr 6910 loss  (to balance).
Reversal (LIFO):  the opposite of the latest month's charge, at its voucher date.
Every JE is balanced by construction and validated again by post_journal_entry.
