## 1. About this policy

StockBolt is an accounting and business management service for startups, entrepreneurs and small businesses, available on the website and as an Android app. The website and the app use the same account and the same data. This policy explains what information StockBolt handles, why, who processes it, and your choices.

## 2. Information we handle

**Your account**
- Name, email address, and a password stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
- Your sign-in session and preferences (such as language, filters and report periods), stored on your device.

**Your business records** (what you and your colleagues enter)
- Company details: name, address, contact details, tax registration and other registration numbers.
- Customers and suppliers: names, contact details, tax numbers.
- Products, stock, quotes, invoices, bills, expenses, payments, bank and cash accounts, accounting entries and reports.
- Payroll, if you use it: employee names, contact details, identity numbers such as Emirates ID, bank account (IBAN) details and salary.
- Files you upload, such as company logos and product images.

These records often contain personal information about your customers, suppliers and employees. Your business decides what to enter and is responsible for having the right to do so.

**Support requests** [FOUNDER: keep this paragraph only once Help & Support is live]
- The subject, description, messages and any screenshots or files you send through Help & Support.

**Technical information**
- Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as IP address, time and request details, for security and reliability.

## 3. Why we use it

- To sign you in and keep your account secure.
- To store and show your company's records, produce documents and reports, and apply the permissions your administrator sets.
- To keep each company's records separate from every other company's.
- To answer support requests.
- To prevent abuse and keep the service running.
- To meet legal obligations.

## 4. AI: how StockBolt is built vs how your data is handled

StockBolt is developed extensively with AI-assisted software development tools (about 99% of the development work, by the founder's estimate). That is about how the software is written.

At the date of this policy, StockBolt does **not** send your account information or business records to any AI service, and no AI model is trained on them. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and tell you what is sent, to whom and why **before** it starts, and ask for consent where the law requires.

## 7. Security

- All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Each company's records are separated at the database level: users only see the company they belong to, with access limited by the roles their administrator sets.
- Payroll details are visible only to company administrators.
- Support screenshots are kept in private storage, available only to the person who raised the request and the StockBolt support team, through short-lived links.
- Our infrastructure provider encrypts stored data [FOUNDER: confirm with Supabase documentation for your plan].

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach affects you, we will act as the law requires.

Access to customer data by the StockBolt operator or support team is limited to providing support, keeping the service running, or meeting legal obligations.

- **Export.** A company administrator can download all of the company's records at any time: Settings → Import / Export → Download my data.
- **Correction.** You can correct most records directly in StockBolt.

- **Account closure.** See the Delete your account page on our website, or use the link in the app's menu. Closing the account of a company owner deletes the company's records; closing a team member's account deletes only that person's login.
