2. THE ENGINE (getTaxReturn) — reuse, no recompute
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Jurisdiction from companies.country_code (IN → IN_GST; else AE_VAT).
  • Tax accounts from the pure map (UAE: 2200 output / 1500 input; India:
    2210/2220/2230 output, 1510/1520/1530 input/ITC) — the seedCOA/seedTaxRates
    accounts, not new logic.
  • output_tax = Σ(credit−debit) over output accounts; input_tax = Σ(debit−credit)
    over input accounts — GL is posted-only by construction (drafts never post,
    voids net to zero). net_payable = output − input.
  • Reconciliation = GL tax movement vs tax carried on posted source documents
    (Σ invoices.tax_amount − Σ credit_notes.tax_amount for output; Σ vendor_bills
    − Σ debit_notes for input), confirmed docs only. Equal by construction unless
    a manual JE hit a tax account or a document's posted tax ≠ its header.
