3. WHAT FAILED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

F1  CROSS-TENANT DATA EXPOSURE — 7 functions          [MANDATORY] [FIXED, PENDING APPLY]
    Seven SECURITY DEFINER functions accepted a p_company_id and never checked
    it against the caller's own tenant. SECURITY DEFINER bypasses RLS, so any
    authenticated user could pass another company's UUID and read or write that
    tenant's data. Confirmed by reading the function bodies, not inferred.

      search_contacts           READ   names, phones, emails, tax IDs, credit limits
      search_products           READ   full catalog including selling prices
      get_bank_recon            READ   bank ledger movements, running balance
      get_daily_cash_report     READ   per-account cash in/out and balances
      save_bank_reconciliation  WRITE  creates and locks a reconciliation
      recompute_stock_valuation WRITE  rewrites derived cost columns
      seed_default_tax_rates    WRITE  inserts tax rates

    This is the most serious defect AC-V found. It is a confidentiality breach
    across tenants in a multi-tenant SaaS, not merely a hardening gap.

    FIXED in phase65 (commit 172313b). NOT YET APPLIED.

F2  ACCOUNTING INVARIANT E1 FAILING — 2 tenants       [MANDATORY] [FIX BUILT, REPAIR NOT RUN]
    verify_invariants reports E1 (Stock Valuation = Inventory 1300) failing for:
      Pro_Parts   596.44   stranded deferred COGS (phase63)
      IMBD123     210.53   flush never written back to subledger (phase64)
    Both engines are fixed and applied; the DATA repairs have not been run.
    Until they are, two live customers carry overstated inventory and
    understated COGS. Pro_Parts additionally shows G2 (negative cash) —
    informational only, not an error.

F3  AUDIT LOG NOT TAMPER-PROOF AGAINST PRIVILEGED CODE [IMPORTANT] [NOT FIXED]

4. CORRECTIONS TO EARLIER REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three claims made earlier in this engagement were wrong and are corrected here.

  1. "Zero RPCs write general_ledger without composing post_journal_entry."
     FALSE. The regex behind that check silently matched nothing. The true
     figure is 46 RPCs writing general_ledger directly, ~30 of which do not
     compose post_journal_entry — including close_fiscal_year (an AC-1 engine).
     This is pre-existing legacy architecture, not a regression, and balance is
     protected by the je_must_balance trigger. Triaged ENHANCEMENT / DEFER.

  2. "Confirmed invoices with no journal entry" (10 documents).
     FALSE POSITIVE. The filter required source_type='invoice'; those invoices
     have been through edit/repost cycles and carry other source types. Each has
     4–14 journal entries and 15–35 GL lines. No corruption.

  3. "11 unpushed commits."
     WRONG. origin/main had already been pushed to 02a4fa2. The count was being
     incremented from a stale reading instead of re-checked.

Also investigated and cleared: reset_company_data looked like the worst possible
offender (SECURITY DEFINER, arbitrary company_id, destructive) but is correctly
guarded — caller tenant, admin role, and an exact company-name confirmation. It
was left untouched and is now locked by a tripwire.

5. NOT TESTED — AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIORITY 1  Engines end-to-end
PRIORITY 6  Reopen workflows
    Both require creating documents, posting them, reversing them and asserting
    the resulting ledger. That means mutating a database. The only configured
    target is production, and all six behavioural suites call
    assertNotProductionTarget() and refuse to run against it. This guard is
    correct and was not bypassed.

PRIORITY 5  Period locking — PARTIAL
    Verified structurally: the posting RPCs that check period_lock_date do so,
    and the lock-setting paths (close_fiscal_year, file_tax_return) are intact.
    NOT verified: that posting into a locked period is actually rejected at
    runtime. Eleven RPCs do not check the lock at all — mostly opening-balance
    and payroll paths where that may be intentional; this needs a behavioural
    test to settle, not a source read. No company currently has a lock set, so
    the question is untested in production data too.

PRIORITY 9  Exports
    The export path (downloadXLSX / downloadCSV) is TypeScript, not SQL, and its
    correctness is about column/row alignment per report. A static sweep cannot
    prove it. This needs either a unit test per report shape or manual
    inspection. Deliberately not attempted rather than claimed.

7. PRODUCTION READINESS ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT: NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION SIGN-OFF.

Three independent blockers, in severity order:

  1. AN OPEN CROSS-TENANT DATA BREACH. Seven functions currently let any
     authenticated user read another company's contacts, catalog, bank ledger
     and cash position, and write to three of their tables. The fix is written,
     committed and verified additive — but it is not applied. Until M1 is done,
     this is live.

  2. TWO CUSTOMERS HAVE KNOWN-WRONG BOOKS. Inventory overstated and COGS
     understated by 596.44 (Pro_Parts) and 210.53 (IMBD123). The engines are
     fixed; the historical data is not.

  3. NO POSTING ENGINE HAS EVER BEEN VALIDATED END-TO-END. Six behavioural
     suites, roughly 50 scenarios, zero executions.

On (3), the evidence is not theoretical. Two live accounting defects were found
during this engagement, both by manually inspecting production data, neither by
the test suite:
   - phase63: the deferred-COGS flush never fired, silently, for weeks.
   - phase64: a defect in the phase63 fix itself, shipped hours earlier. Tests
     D1–D8 could not catch it because they all assert the general ledger, and
     the ledger was already correct — the defect lived in the subledger.
The system's own tests have never once caught an accounting defect. Every one
was found by hand. That is the honest state of assurance today.

WHAT IS GENUINELY STRONG:
  - The data is internally consistent. Every structural and corruption check
    passed across all 11 tenants with zero exceptions.
  - Trial balance nets to zero everywhere; no unbalanced journal entry exists.
  - Structure, permissions and composition are continuously enforced by 136
    tripwires against the live database, gated by a pre-commit hook.
  - Migrations are additive, idempotent, hand-applied, and patched from live
    function definitions rather than from files that may have drifted.

The foundation is sound and the arithmetic is unit-tested. What is missing is
proof that the engines behave correctly when actually run.
