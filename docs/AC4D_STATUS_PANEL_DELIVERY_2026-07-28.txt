--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The increment that makes AC-4 usable end-to-end. On a CONFIRMED sales invoice in
a supported jurisdiction (India / UAE), a panel that:
  • Generate — assembles the real invoice/customer/company/product rows into the
    canonical model → runs the AC-4B formatter → AC-4C record RPC.
  • View / Download — shows the payload and downloads it (.json / .xml).
  • Mark submitted — records the manual government reference (IRN / ack / QR).
  • Cancel — cancels the active document.
  • History — lists superseded / cancelled snapshots.
All write actions are gated by useHasPermission('sales.write'); nothing touches
the GL. Readiness is WARN-BUT-ALLOW (missing fields shown; Generate still works).
